KeepSift Support for Mac

Updated October 8, 2026

KeepSift helps you review and organize photos on your Mac. For help, email Desheng Wang at desheng666@outlook.com. Please include the KeepSift version, your macOS version, what you were trying to do, and the exact error message if one appeared.

Local Photos folder access

If KeepSift says it can no longer access a local photo folder, return to Library, select Local Photos, and choose the same folder again. This renews macOS permission without intentionally clearing your review history. Do not manually move files in KeepSift's data or quarantine folders.

Apple Photos and iCloud

Check that KeepSift has Photos permission in macOS System Settings. In Library, choose Apple Photos, set an import period, and refresh that period. Photos stored only in iCloud may need time and an internet connection to download through Apple's Photos service. If you need to clear temporary image renditions, finish or pause the current photo task first, then use Settings → Clear Download Cache. This does not delete photos from Apple Photos.

KeepSift Pro purchases

If Pro does not unlock after a purchase, open KeepSift's purchase controls and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests through its App Store support channels. Do not email payment card details or Apple Account credentials.

Cleanup and recovery

Keep/Reject decisions only record review choices. Cleanup has a separate preview and confirmation. For a local-folder cleanup, use KeepSift's Restore feature while the quarantined files are still available. For Apple Photos removals, look in Photos → Recently Deleted; KeepSift cannot restore those items directly.

Safe diagnostics

Settings → Export Diagnostics creates an optional archive of technical information without photos, file paths, database contents, face data, People names, credentials, or application log contents. Export Logs is separate and may include private paths, filenames, and error details. Review any file before attaching it to an email. KeepSift does not upload diagnostics or logs automatically.

For a suspected security or privacy issue, email the support address above rather than posting private details publicly. See the KeepSift Privacy Policy for Mac for more information.

# KeepSift Privacy Policy 
 
**Effective date: August 12, 2026** 
 
KeepSift is a desktop photo organization and review application published by Joey Wang Software. 
 
## Data Access and Processing 
 
KeepSift accesses only the photos and folders that you choose to open. 
 
To provide photo organization, comparison, duplicate detection, face-aware review, and photo selection features, KeepSift may locally process information including: 
 
* Image content 
* File names and file paths 
* Image metadata, including EXIF information 
* Thumbnails and image previews 
* Image similarity information 
* Detected faces and facial regions 
* Locally generated facial feature data used for photo comparison and organization 
* Photo quality and analysis results 
 
## Local Processing 
 
Photo and facial analysis is performed locally on your Windows device. 
 
KeepSift does not upload your photos, facial analysis data, image metadata, thumbnails, or analysis results to Joey Wang Software or to external servers. 
 
Facial analysis is used only to provide local photo organization and review features, such as identifying similar faces across photos, evaluating facial quality, and helping compare related photographs. 
 
## Data Collection and Sharing 
 
KeepSift does not require a user account. 
 
Joey Wang Software does not receive or remotely collect your photos, facial analysis data, image metadata, file information, or locally generated analysis results. 
 
KeepSift does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties and does not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or tracking. 
 
## Local Storage 
 
KeepSift may store application data locally on your device, including: 
 
* Application preferences 
* Project and library information 
* Thumbnails and previews 
* Image fingerprints and similarity information 
* Photo quality analysis results 
* Facial analysis results and locally generated face representations 
 
This locally stored information is used to improve application performance and avoid unnecessary repeated analysis. 
 
You can remove locally stored KeepSift data by deleting the relevant project or application data. Uninstalling KeepSift may also remove application-managed data, subject to normal Windows application and user-data behavior. 
 
KeepSift does not intentionally modify your original photo files unless a feature explicitly informs you that it will do so. 
 
## Third-Party Services 
 
KeepSift does not use third-party advertising, analytics, cloud photo storage, or user-tracking services. 
 
Photo analysis does not require photos or facial analysis data to be sent to third-party cloud services. 
 
## Children’s Privacy 
 
KeepSift is a general-purpose photo organization utility and is not specifically directed to children. 
 
Joey Wang Software does not knowingly collect personal information from children through KeepSift. 
 
## Security 
 
Because KeepSift performs photo analysis locally, your photos and locally generated analysis data remain on your device unless you choose to move, copy, share, or otherwise export them using KeepSift, Windows, or another application. 
 
## Changes to This Policy 
 
This Privacy Policy may be updated when KeepSift's features or data practices change. 
 
Any updated version will include a revised effective date.

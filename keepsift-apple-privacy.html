KeepSift Privacy Policy for Mac

Effective October 8, 2026

KeepSift is a local-first photo organization application published by Desheng Wang. This policy describes the Mac App Store version of KeepSift.

Photos and information processed on your Mac

KeepSift accesses a local folder only after you select it. If you choose Apple Photos, macOS asks you to authorize access to your Photos library. KeepSift uses the photos you make available to organize and compare images, suggest picks, find duplicates and photo issues, review people, and prepare optional edits and cleanup.

Depending on the features you use, on-device processing may include image content; file names and paths; dates and other image metadata (which may contain camera location information); thumbnails and temporary previews; image fingerprints and quality scores; face locations, landmarks, and face-derived representations; People clusters and names you assign; and review or cleanup decisions. People Discovery asks for in-app consent before starting new face-recognition analysis.

Local storage and network services

KeepSift stores its settings, library catalog, analysis results, People data, review history, and application logs on your Mac. In the sandboxed Mac App Store version, an opaque macOS security-scoped bookmark may be stored so KeepSift can reopen a folder you selected. For Apple Photos, KeepSift may keep bounded temporary image renditions on your Mac to avoid downloading them repeatedly; you can clear this download cache in Settings. Local logs can include file names, paths, and error details.

KeepSift does not automatically send your photos, thumbnails, photo paths, face-derived data, People names, or analysis results to the publisher. The Mac app does not use advertising or third-party analytics and does not sell your personal information. Its photo and face analysis runs on your Mac, not on the publisher's servers.

If you use Apple Photos with iCloud Photos enabled, Apple may download photos to your Mac or synchronize changes under your Apple account. KeepSift does not operate iCloud Photos; Apple's handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Optional KeepSift Pro purchases and restores use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. KeepSift checks the product and entitlement status needed for those features and does not add your photos or face-analysis data to purchase requests. Apple handles purchase and account information under its own policies.

Cleanup and exports

KeepSift does not automatically delete your original photos. A local-folder cleanup move requires a separate preview and confirmation and can be restored through KeepSift while the quarantined files remain available. An Apple Photos removal requires its own confirmation and may synchronize through iCloud; recovery is handled by Photos' Recently Deleted feature, subject to Apple's rules. Exporting an edited photo creates a new file rather than overwriting the source.

Diagnostics and support

KeepSift does not automatically upload diagnostics or logs. If you choose Export Diagnostics, the resulting archive contains technical version and environment information but excludes photos, photo paths, database contents, face data, People names, credentials, and log contents. Export Logs is a separate, explicitly confirmed action; exported logs may contain file paths, photo file names, account or folder names, and error details. Review any archive before deciding to send it.

If you voluntarily email the publisher a support request, screenshots, diagnostics, or logs, the publisher will use that material to respond to your request and for related support or legal obligations. Please do not send original photos, passwords, purchase credentials, or other sensitive material unless specifically needed. Your email provider and the publisher's email provider will process the message under their own policies. To request deletion of material you sent for support, contact the address below.

Your choices

You can change or revoke access to Photos in macOS Settings, choose a different local folder, clear the Apple Photos download cache, delete People Discovery data, and uninstall KeepSift. Revoking Photos permission or uninstalling the app may not by itself erase all previously stored application data; you can remove remaining KeepSift data from your Mac after restoring any quarantined photos you want to keep. Photos in Apple's Recently Deleted collection must be recovered through Photos while Apple still makes them available.

Children and changes

KeepSift is a general-purpose photo tool and is not directed to children. The publisher does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. This policy will be updated if KeepSift's data practices change; the effective date above will identify the current version.
